Hackathon Brief: Build the Online Store

BharatStore: A retail chain that has always sold through physical stores expanding across India and moving into online shopping.

All 3 Aiven Services Active

The Challenge: A retail chain that has always sold through physical stores is expanding across India and moving into online shopping. The business wants a storefront that is fast, stays accurate when stock changes, and helps shoppers find what they are looking for without any fuss across Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Kolkata.

The Solution: Everything runs on Aiven. We combined Aiven PostgreSQL (the store's memory & zero overselling), Aiven Apache Kafka (real-time order events & inventory decrement), and Aiven OpenSearch (the typo-tolerant search bar).

1. PostgreSQL

The Store's Memory & Truth

Maintains tables for products, inventory, customers, orders, and order items. Uses BEGIN ... SELECT stock FROM inventory WHERE product_id = %s FOR UPDATE ... COMMIT to guarantee that if 10 customers buy the last item simultaneously, stock never drops below zero.

2. Apache Kafka

The Event Stream Broker

Emits transactional ORDER_CREATED events to the order-events topic. A dedicated background consumer worker group processes events asynchronously: decrements warehouse inventory, dispatches customer confirmations, and keeps OpenSearch in sync.

3. OpenSearch

The Typo-Tolerant Search Bar

Ingests the full product catalog into the bharatkart-products index. Implements OpenSearch Multi-Match with fuzziness: "AUTO" so common typos like "lapop", "bluetoth", or "samsng" return accurate products, with filters for category, price, and regional fulfillment hubs.
